Overview

What Is This Platform?

The Yeng Constantino Official platform is a custom-built fan hub that brings together everything Yeng — her music catalog, fan community, exclusive merchandise, tiered membership, and direct engagement tools.

It serves as the central destination for Yeng Nation, replacing fragmented social media with a unified, owned platform.

Key Highlights

  • 9-page responsive website
  • Full music archive with video + audio
  • Fan community with posts, fan art, covers
  • 3-tier membership system
  • Merch store with order management
  • Admin dashboard with analytics
  • Multi-language support (4 languages)

Features

Core Features

🎧

Music & Video Archive

Complete discography browser with video cards (play overlay, YouTube links) and audio cards (Spotify/YouTube). Filter by era, category, mood. Fan rating system (1-10).

🛒

Merch Store

Full e-commerce with product catalog, size/variant selection, cart, checkout, order tracking. Admin manages products via Airtable.

⭐

Membership Tiers

3-tier system (Free / Sariwang Himig / Ikaw Lamang) with exclusive content, early access, discounts, and community perks.

🎤

Yeng Covers

Fan cover submission portal with YouTube link integration, admin review, featuring system, and Hall of Fame. Leaderboard-style rankings.

💬

Community

Fan posts with categories (Post, Fan Art, Cover Video, Memory, Question). Like system, admin moderation, engagement tools.

📸

Instagram Integration

Live Instagram feed grid on homepage and community page. Admin-managed via Site Config with simple URL input.
